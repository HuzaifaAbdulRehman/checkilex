# Release scope

Prepared 14 October 2026 for Huzaifa Abdul Rehman's local prototype.
Owner release approval is pending.

The initial operator and intended tester location is Pakistan. Broader
distribution jurisdictions have not been assessed. This is free source software,
with no hosted service, accounts, paid orders, subscriptions, employee access,
marketing system or business processing agreement.

A user may paste client text, so personal data is possible. Processing happens
in the user's local app, local Ollama runtime and fresh browser contexts.
The maintainer does not receive that text through the application. No history,
telemetry, cookies or browser storage is implemented. Explicit exports remain
under the user's control; clearing UI state is not secure erasure.

MIT terms and the local-data notice are prepared beside this record.
Dependency/model licences are documented in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md.
No model training, remote inference, regulated decision or acceptance
certification is offered. The synthetic café is original demonstration content.

Before public distribution, the owner must approve the release package and its
support channel. These engineering records do not establish legal sufficiency.
